The three directions

Same club, same facts, three different stories. Each is built around three signature interactions.

What we found

Before designing, we went through your current site, the 2017 archive, Justdial, FITPASS, Google-listing mirrors, Instagram, Facebook and Dehradun gym round-ups.

What the new site fixes

  • 01Find us. There's no street address, map, hours or tap-to-call on the current site. Every direction now has all of them.
  • 02Proof. Your 4.7★ and 4.8★ ratings, real member words and published results now show up front instead of being missing.
  • 03Your own photography. The current gallery leads with text-heavy posters. These directions lead with the floor itself: the neon, the hex lights and four floors of equipment.
  • 04Classes people can plan around. Each direction has a timetable, and you can book a trial straight from a class.
  • 05Enquiries that land. The booking flows work on WhatsApp and calendar today. In the full build they become real leads.

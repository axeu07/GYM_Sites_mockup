The three directions

One club, three styles, all built on joy, power, tribe and restore.

What we found

Before designing, we went through your poster, photos and videos, Google and Justdial listings and reviews, Instagram and Facebook.

What the new site fixes

  • 01Everything under one roof. Strength, cardio, boxing, Zumba, Bhangra, yoga, steam and a cafeteria. Most Jakhan gyms are just a workout floor, so every direction leads with this.
  • 02The boxing club. Your strongest difference. It gets its own section or chapter in every direction.
  • 03Premium, but local. Steam room, premium washrooms and a cafeteria, at a price reviewers call affordable.
  • 04A tribe, not just a gym. The Morning Grind and Shikhar Falls treks: the community that keeps people coming back.
  • 05Leads that land. Every direction books a free trial or opens WhatsApp with the details already filled in.

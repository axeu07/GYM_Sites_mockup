      I

      入門 · Before you begin

      A letter to the new member.

      Jakhan, Dehradun

      Dear future member,

      Ikigai (生き甲斐) is Japanese for “a reason for being”. Iki means life and gai means worth. It sits where four circles overlap: what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs and what you need.

      that's the whole idea

      We built the club the same way. Joy is the studio: Zumba, Bhangra and yoga. Power is the floor: racks, plates, air bikes and a boxing club of its own. Tribe is the people: Morning Grind sessions and treks into the hills. Restore is the steam room, the showers and the cafeteria.

      There's a line painted on our wall: this place will change you, if you let it. Nobody here expects a six-week miracle. Come a little more often, lift a little better, and keep coming back.

      Come and see it. Your first day is on us.

      The Ikigai team IKIGAI Fitness Club · Jakhan, Dehradun

02 / 生き甲斐 The philosophy

Four circles.
One reason. Yours.

Ikigai is Japanese for “a reason for being”. It sits where four circles overlap: what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs and what you need. The club is built the same way. One floor of Doon Zone Tower holds four worlds.

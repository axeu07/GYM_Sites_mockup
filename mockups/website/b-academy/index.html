      I

      Principal's note

      A note from the Director.

      Laxman Chowk, Dehradun

      Dear future member,

      Fitness is not a punishment. It's a practice. We have believed that since the day we opened our doors at Laxman Chowk in 2009 — back when one of the city's first treadmills stood on our floor.

      where it all began

      No random workouts. No copy-pasted routines. Every plan here begins with a conversation, not a clipboard: your history, your old injuries, your actual week, and what “fit” means to you. Then a coach who knows exactly when to push and when to hold back.

      The goal is not a six-week spike. It is strength and habits that keep working long after the photographs stop. Members who pause — for work, for travel, for life — come back. We take that as the best review there is.

      Come and see the campus. Your first session is on us.

      Shobhit Mittal Director, Fitness Solutions

A website for **IKIGAI Fitness Club**, a unisex, women-friendly gym in Jakhan, Rajpur Road, Dehradun. The site should feel personal and premium, and it runs on one lead flow: **book a free trial or message on WhatsApp**.

## 3. Positioning
1. **Everything under one roof:** strength, cardio, boxing, dance (Zumba, Bhangra), yoga, recovery (steam) and a cafeteria.
2. **A boxing club inside the gym:** the strongest differentiator, with its own section.
3. **A premium experience at a local price:** steam room, cafeteria and premium washrooms, yet reviewers call it affordable.
4. **Community, not just a gym:** Morning Grind socials and treks.
5. **Local soul:** Bhangra and Doon energy; the personal alternative to the chains arriving in the area.
6. **Women-friendly and hygienic**, said plainly.

## 4. Concept: "Four circles, one reason"
*Ikigai* (生き甲斐) is Japanese for "a reason for being": *iki* means life and *gai* means worth.

| Circle | Pillar | Contents | Kanji used |
|---|---|---|---|
| What you love | **Joy** | Zumba, Bhangra, Yoga | 喜 Yorokobi |
| What you're good at | **Power** | Strength, lifting, cardio, Boxing | 力 Chikara |
| What the world needs | **Tribe** | Morning Grind, treks, community | 仲間 Nakama |
| What you need | **Restore** | Steam, showers, lockers, cafeteria | 癒し Iyashi |
| Centre | **Ikigai** | "Find your reason to move." | 生き甲斐 |

Lines: *Find your reason to move.* · "Every rep has a reason." · "Iki = life. Gai = worth." · "Four circles. One reason. Yours." · "Find your Ikigai."
Japanese words are accents only and always appear beside the English.

**Visual system:** deep black `#0A0A0A` · washi paper `#F2EEE6` · ember `#FF5A1F` (the single accent) · ember glow `#FF8A3D` · ember deep `#C2410C` · hanko red `#D7261E` (stamps and kanji) · ash `#8A8580` · steam gold `#C9A36A`. Kanji in Noto Serif JP. The hexagon pattern from the club's branding appears as a faint texture.

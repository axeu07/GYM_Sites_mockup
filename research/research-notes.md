## What the current site gets wrong (and the mockups fix)
- **Missing basics:** no street address, map, hours, `tel:` link, social links or reviews.
- **Team and results lack substance:**
  - Team cards are empty numbered boxes.
  - Transformations have no photos or quotes.
  - Classes have no schedule.
- **Weak gallery:** the teaser shows text-heavy social posters instead of the gym's own good photography.
- **SEO problems:**
  - Client-side rendering only, so crawlers see an empty page.
  - No Open Graph or schema.
  - Every unknown URL returns a blank 200 page.
- **Off-brand details:**
  - The favicon is a generic builder icon.
  - The logo is low-resolution.
  - The site's yellow differs from the logo's yellow.
